A temple of fire at the foot of Arunachala

Arunachaleswarar Temple in Thiruvannamalai is one of the five Pancha Bhoota Sthalams, where Shiva is worshipped as fire. Pilgrims come for darshan, for the Karthigai Deepam lamp on the hilltop, and to walk the Girivalam path around the hill.

Girivalam: 14 km around the sacred hill

Walk clockwise from the temple, past the eight Ashta Lingams, and return to where you started. Most walkers need four to six hours.
